Meet Our Writers

About Us

Senior Angler was created for the generation that grew up with a rod in hand and a worm on a hook. We are a small, independent blog based in the United States, dedicated to the unique joys and challenges of fishing in your retirement years.

Why We Exist

Most fishing content online moves too fast—flashy boats, expensive sonar, and high-energy tournaments. We wanted a place where the pace matches the quiet rhythm of a senior’s fishing trip. Here, we talk about comfortable access, lightweight gear, and the sheer pleasure of a still morning on the water.

Our Writers

Our contributors are seasoned anglers, many of whom have been fishing for over 50 years. We are not professional tournament chasers; we are retirees, grandparents, and lifelong hobbyists who want to share what we’ve learned. Our advice is practical, tested over decades, and always focused on safety and enjoyment.

Our Promise

We do not sell fishing licenses, guide services, or physical products. We are an informational blog. If we recommend a product, it is because one of our writers has used it personally. Our goal is to build a trustworthy, calm corner of the internet for the senior fishing community.

Thank you for stopping by. We’re glad you’re here.
